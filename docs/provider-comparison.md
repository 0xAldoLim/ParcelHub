Reviewed **9 November 2026** against official documentation and the official Klik Resi SDK. No account credentials or real private shipments were supplied. “Documented” below means the contract was checked, not that live access passed.

## Klik Resi

The official SDK provides `client.tracking.get(awb, courier, { number })`, account testing through `client.me.get()`, parsed event dates, and status/history/origin/destination results. ID Express uses code `ide` and requires a phone number. SDK constants also include jne, jnt, tiki, spx, sicepat, ninja, pos, sap, lex, lion and anteraja. ParcelHub maps its internal `idexpress` to `ide`. Actual courier access is account-dependent. [Official SDK](https://github.com/klikresi/node-sdk), [SDK types](https://github.com/klikresi/node-sdk/blob/main/src/types.ts), [tracking implementation](https://github.com/klikresi/node-sdk/blob/main/src/tracking.ts).

The public developer page advertises pay-as-you-go tracking at Rp15/request, charging only HTTP-200 requests, and self-service registration. Numeric request/second or daily limits and redistribution rights were not established from the reviewed page. Configure budgets and confirm intended use/terms with the provider. The SDK's endpoint is `https://klikresi.com/api/trackings/{awb}/couriers/{code}`; SDK handles authentication and envelope parsing. Its supported fields do not include event locations or a distinct city, so ParcelHub does not fabricate those. [Developer offering](https://klikresi.com/developer), [API documentation](https://docs.klikresi.com/).

## RajaOngkir / Komerce

`POST https://rajaongkir.komerce.id/api/v1/track/waybill` authenticates with header `key`. Parameters are `awb`, `courier`, and `last_phone_number` where required; JNE requires the final five recipient digits. The response includes `meta`, `data.summary`, `data.manifest`, and delivery information. ParcelHub submits form-urlencoded parameters and validates identity/history. Documented manifest dates lack an offset; the adapter explicitly labels its Asia/Jakarta UTC+07 interpretation. Only explicitly mapped provider statuses are normalized. Allowed courier codes are configured through `RAJAONGKIR_CARRIERS`; documentation examples do not prove all couriers are included in a user's plan. [Tracking reference](https://www.rajaongkir.com/docs/shipping-cost/tracking), [base URL](https://www.rajaongkir.com/docs/shipping-cost/getting_started/endpoint).

The pricing page distinguishes free Starter and paid Pro/Enterprise plans. Its advertised daily HIT allowances are labeled **Cek Ongkir API**, and do not establish a free AWB allowance or AWB request/second limit. Account tracking entitlement, quotas, current price and personal/commercial use terms remain to be confirmed. The configured per-request cost in ParcelHub is a user-adjustable conservative estimate, not an assertion of RajaOngkir billing. [Official pricing](https://www.rajaongkir.com/pricing).

## Biteship

The reviewed current reference explicitly restricts `GET https://api.biteship.com/v1/trackings/:id` to tracking IDs generated by Biteship orders. ParcelHub therefore requires the optional Biteship tracking ID on a parcel and validates the returned AWB. It never substitutes an external AWB into the order-specific endpoint or scrapes the consumer tracking page. Responses contain status, courier, waybill_id and history note/status/updated_at. [Retrieve a Tracking](https://biteship.com/en/docs/api/trackings/retrieve), [authentication](https://biteship.com/en/docs/api/authentication).

Official plans currently list monthly tracking quotas of 0/2,000/10,000 for Starter/Grow/Scale, with Rp10/request tracking overage from wallet balance. Account plan and installed couriers control availability. Test keys simulate responses and do not verify live shipments. Numeric requests/second limits and broader redistribution permission were not verified. ParcelHub requires a configured cost estimate and opt-in real calls, even when a plan includes requests. [Plans and limits](https://biteship.com/en/docs/plans).

## BinderByte alternative

The official API accepts `api_key`, `courier`, and `awb`; JNE can require `number` for phone verification. It documents 28 courier codes, including the twelve requested families (`ide` for ID Express), summary/detail/history response fields, courier availability discovery, and a quota/balance endpoint. Entitlement and over-quota pricing depend on the account. No fixed free allowance, universal price, numeric rate limit, or redistribution permission is inferred from an example response. It is documented for future adapter work; ParcelHub does not currently dispatch to it. [Official tracking, courier and quota documentation](https://docs.binderbyte.com/api/cek-resi).

## Manual tracking verification

The following official pages were opened or found in primary-source search results and show tracking entry points. ParcelHub opens them without an AWB query parameter; automatic prefilling and successful tracking have not been tested or claimed.

- [JNE](https://www.jne.co.id/tracking-package)
- [J&T](https://www.jet.co.id/track) — phone verification can apply.
- [TIKI](https://www.tiki.id/en/track)
- [SPX](https://spx.co.id/en)
- [AnterAja](https://anteraja.id/tracking)
- [Ninja Xpress](https://www.ninjavan.co/id-id/tracking)

The registry intentionally leaves other official tracking URLs unset when they could not be sufficiently verified during research. Courier/number storage remains available. No CAPTCHA, authentication or verification challenge is bypassed.

## Operational defaults and verification boundary

ParcelHub's concurrency (one request/provider), 60-second manual cooldown, 100/day, 1,000/month, and Rp50,000/month defaults are **application policies**, not provider-published limits. Paid calls and paid fallback are disabled initially. Real adapters are tested against fixtures and error simulations; only a live account test/real parcel response can establish actual connectivity. Provider availability, account rights, quotas, terms, and prices should be reconfirmed before enabling paid calls.
